ScanSolve is a business tool for facilities staff. Accounts are created at scansolve.co; the app is for managing issues reported in the customer's buildings.

Demo sign-in: enter appreview@scansolve.co, tap Send Magic Code, then enter the code [REVIEW_CODE] and tap Verify & Sign In. No email is actually sent to this address.

The demo organisation has four locations and five sample issues. To try the scanner, tap Scan and point it at the attached QR label image; it opens the label's setup page. To log an issue without a label, tap Log issue on the Issues page. Account deletion is in Settings, Delete account. Please test it last; we recreate the demo account afterwards.

Subscriptions are bought on our website by the customer's organisation. The app has no purchasing and doesn't link to it.
