# Vertical brief — Manufacturing & plant

Slug: `manufacturing` · Wave 1 (deep) · Last researched 2026-07-06

## Buyer
Site Maintenance Manager, Plant Facilities Manager. Judged on uptime and audit-readiness.

## The sharp angle
The code on the machine names the exact asset, so the technician goes straight to it instead
of hunting the line — and every report is timestamped for the health-and-safety file. Honesty:
ScanSolve speeds reporting, location and the record; it is not predictive maintenance and does
not prevent failure. It pairs with a CMMS, it doesn't replace one.

## Pain, in their words (sourced)
- Equipment failure is ~42% of all unplanned downtime — the single largest cause; equipment
  plus aging infrastructure is over 70% of stoppages. [src: Aberdeen / industry downtime data]
- Vague reports ("line two's playing up") add minutes of hunting per fault, and downtime is
  paid for by the minute (large-scale manufacturing ≈ $260k/hour). [src: Aberdeen]
- No timestamped record for H&S/audit; verbal shift handovers lose detail.

## Concrete faults
Conveyor slipping · HVAC unit overheating · guard rail loose · compressor leaking · pallet
wrapper jammed · emergency stop faulty · coolant low · forklift charger down.

## Proof stat
"Equipment failure causes 42% of unplanned downtime" — tie to ScanSolve's real benefit: every
minute finding which asset failed is downtime. [src: Aberdeen / industry downtime data]

## Competitor teardown → gap
Per-user CMMS (MaintainX $16–45/user, UpKeep $20–45/user) gets expensive if every operator
needs a licence to report. With ScanSolve the operators who scan aren't users — flat price.
