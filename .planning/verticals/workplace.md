# Vertical brief — Offices & workplace

Slug: `workplace` · Wave 1 (deep) · Last researched 2026-07-06

## Buyer
Regional Facilities Manager, Director of Workplace Experience, office manager. Judged on
employee experience and time-to-resolution; holds discretionary pilot budget. This is the
lead vertical — the no-login scan-and-report is the core product, ready to demo today.

## The sharp angle
Remove the reporting friction. Employees won't log into an intranet portal, find the right
category, and file a form for a broken coffee machine — so faults surface as complaints, late.
A QR at the fault means anyone reports it in seconds, no login. Plus the flat-price wedge vs
per-seat CMMS.

## Pain, in their words (sourced)
- "When employees can report issues easily, they're more likely to report them" — i.e. hard
  reporting means faults go unlogged and facilities lose visibility. [src: Envoy]
- Real daily faults: "the light in a meeting room won't turn on, the Wi-Fi might go out, the
  toilet won't stop flushing, the sliding door gets jammed." [src: Envoy]
- Portal friction is the barrier: a clunky/confusing ticket system in week one is a red flag,
  and employees don't know what's happening with their ticket. [src: Envoy]

## Concrete faults
Coffee machine down · meeting room AV not connecting · aircon too warm · printer jammed · desk
height stuck · kitchen tap leaking · booth light out · access door not reading.

## Proof / insight (no fabricated number)
Something in the office is always broken; the reason it doesn't get fixed fast is that
reporting means opening a portal nobody wants to open. Frame as insight, not a stat.

## Competitor teardown → gap
- CMMS / workplace platforms bill per user: MaintainX $16–41/user/mo, UpKeep $20–45/user/mo,
  workplace platforms $20–100/user/mo. [src: MaintainX/UpKeep/Facilio pricing]
- Envoy and similar workplace-ticketing tools solve the routing but still sit behind a login.
- Gap: the reporter needs no login and no per-seat bill. ScanSolve is flat for the whole team,
  and the person scanning never signs in.
