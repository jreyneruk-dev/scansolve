# Vertical brief — Financial services

Slug: `financial-services` · Wave 1 (deep) · Pursue last (features thinnest) · Last researched 2026-07-06

## Buyer
Director of Facilities Management, Building Manager. Judged on holding outsourced vendors
(ISS, Mitie, Sodexo, CBRE) to SLA and proving cost efficiency.

## The sharp angle
An independent, timestamped record of every fault — raised to resolved — that you control,
separate from the vendor's own system. Honesty: ScanSolve gives the record + Insights
(resolution time by location) + CSV export; it does NOT ship a built vendor-scorecard/SLA-timer
or per-area cost dashboard. Frame around the record, not unbuilt analytics.

## Pain, in their words (sourced)
- 60–70% of FM work is run through contracted providers, yet fewer than 30% of operations have
  a documented SLA measuring contractor performance. [src: FM vendor-management data]
- The vendor owns the only record of raise/resolve, so SLA reviews run on anecdote.
- Duplicate call-outs: tracking first-time-fix per vendor cut callback rate 68% in 30 days. [src: oxmaint]

## Concrete faults

lighting fault · access barrier jammed · vending machine down · carpet trip hazard.

## Proof stat
"60–70% of FM work is outsourced. Fewer than 30% is measured against an SLA." → without your own
record, the vendor's numbers are the only numbers. [src: FM vendor-management data]

## Competitor teardown → gap
Not a per-seat CMMS play. The wedge is an independent record for one flat price, sitting
alongside the FM provider's system — not another per-head SaaS bill on top of the contract.
