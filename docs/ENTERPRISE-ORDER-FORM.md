# Enterprise order form — template

Internal template. Fill in, send as PDF, get it signed. **Not a public page.**

The point of this document is that it is short. It varies only what must vary — price,
term, scope, response times — and incorporates the published terms by reference. Do not
write a bespoke contract per customer: you cannot track a portfolio of divergent
obligations, and the first customer's negotiated clause becomes everyone's expectation.

**Before sending, check:**
- Scope is measured, not estimated. The pilot ends with an exact commissioned-location count — use it.
- Nothing unbuilt is promised. If a feature is not shipped, it goes in the roadmap table with a date, or it does not appear.
- The renewal price is on the form, even when year one is comped.

## Notes for the founder (delete before sending)

**On comping year one.** Comp at full list value, never discount. Both cost the same in
cash. A comped £7,200 contract puts £7,200 on the order form and in the case study; a
discounted £1,200 contract sets £1,200 as your permanent anchor — through case-study
numbers, through facilities directors talking to each other, and through your own
willingness to ask next time. Always fill in the renewal fee, or you have built a £0 to
£7,200 cliff nobody renews across.

**On their paper.** If a large customer insists on their own contract, that is normal.
At this deal size it is usually not worth the legal spend — "here are our standard terms"
is a legitimate answer. If you do accept their paper, get it reviewed; the clauses that
matter are liability cap, indemnity, termination for convenience, and any uptime
commitment you cannot meet.

**On the liability cap.** Our terms cap liability at fees paid in the prior 12 months. Do
not agree to uncapped liability, and do not agree to an uptime SLA with service credits
until there is monitoring to measure it.

**Not legal advice.** This template was drafted in-house. Have a solicitor review it
before the first signature, particularly the sections a university or FM contractor will
mark up.
